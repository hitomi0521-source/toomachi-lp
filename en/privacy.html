PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy policy

The Toomachi Tourism Association ("we") handles the personal information you give us when you reserve a seat or ask about stays for Toomachi Summer Fireworks Night as follows.

1. What we collect

Seat reservations
Name, email, phone number, number of people, questions or requests (optional)
Questions about stays on LINE
Your LINE display name and what you ask us (inn, dates, number of people, etc.)
Email inquiries
Email address, name and what you ask us

We don't collect more than we need to handle your booking or question.

2. How we use it

  • To take your reservation, send the confirmation email and check you in on the day
  • To contact you about the event, such as a postponement or cancellation due to bad weather
  • To handle cancellations and answer questions
  • To answer questions about stays (rooms and plans)

We don't use it for anything else, such as advertising.

3. Sharing with others

We don't share your information with anyone else without your consent, unless required by law. If we need to tell an inn your name, number of people and dates to check availability, we will ask you first.

4. Storage and how long we keep it

  • Reservations are managed with Google services (Sheets and Gmail), and only the staff in charge can see them.
  • Questions about stays are handled through our official LINE account (a service of LY Corporation).
  • We delete your information within 3 months after the event.

5. Requests and contact

To see, correct or delete your information, please contact us below. English is OK.
